AeroMesh: A Self-Healing Aerial Edge Mesh for Post-Disaster Connectivity and On-Board Damage Assessment

Ground responders, a relay backbone of UAVs, and on-board inference nodes that defer uncertain tiles to a larger model over the mesh — coordinated by policy from a ground control station rather than direct command.

Ground layer

Responder teams, a field medical post, and community hotspots feed traffic into two gateways by priority (0 = highest). A satellite link provides opportunistic backhaul at the incident-command gateway.

Relay backbone

Three relay UAVs form the self-healing mesh backbone, bridging each ground gateway into the aerial layer so no single link failure isolates a ground node.

Inference layer

Inference UAV 1 runs an early-exit DNN with uncertainty quantification on degraded imagery; uncertain tiles are deferred over the mesh to Inference UAV 2's larger model, which also ingests features from Inference UAV 3's sensing pass.

Control plane

The ground control station sets mission goals and classification policy only — it issues policy and bids, not direct routing commands, keeping the mesh's healing behavior local and autonomous.
